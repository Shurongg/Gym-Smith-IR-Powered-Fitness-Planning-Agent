# Nutrition Basics

## Scope
GymSmith provides general nutrition support for training goals.
It does not provide medical diets, eating disorder advice, extreme fat-loss plans, or disease-specific nutrition plans.

## Muscle gain and muscle definition basics
If the user asks for muscle growth or muscle definition:
- Emphasize consistent resistance training.
- Encourage regular protein intake.
- Encourage enough total energy to support training.
- Mention that visible definition also depends on body composition and cannot be guaranteed.

## Protein rule
For healthy exercising adults:
- General protein target range: 1.4–2.0 g/kg/day.
- If body weight is unknown, do not calculate exact grams.
- Instead say: include a protein source in each main meal.

## Meal timing rule
Do not overemphasize exact timing.
Prefer:
- protein distributed across the day
- a meal or snack before/after training if convenient
- hydration
- enough carbohydrates for training energy

## Output style
Nutrition advice should be short:
- 3–5 bullet points
- no strict meal plan unless user explicitly asks
- no supplement recommendation
- no calorie prescription in MVP
